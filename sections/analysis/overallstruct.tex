\section{Analysis}

Analysis is organized as a progression from foundations to topology, convergence, calculus, and finally geometric and measure-theoretic analysis. The foundations part fixes language, sets, functions, number systems, completeness, and cardinality. The topological part studies metric spaces, compactness, connectedness, completeness, and continuity. The convergence part develops sequences, series, uniform convergence, function spaces, power series, approximation, and Fourier ideas. The calculus part treats differentiation and integration in one and several variables. The final part connects differential forms, Stokes theory, measure, and Lebesgue integration.
