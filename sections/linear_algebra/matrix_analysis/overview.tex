\section{Matrix Analysis and Numerical Structure}

Matrix analysis translates structural, geometric, and spectral theory into quantitative matrix behavior and computation. The section first treats trace, determinant, rank, spectral radius, positive semidefinite and positive definite matrices, idempotents, nilpotents, and structured matrix products; then develops LU, QR, Cholesky, SVD, compact SVD, polar decomposition, and matrix modulus; then studies spectral and Frobenius norms, singular-value inequalities, low-rank approximation, pseudoinverses, conditioning, and norm-based error bounds; then gathers perturbation and stability tools such as Bauer--Fike, Gersgorin, diagonal dominance, Rayleigh quotients, interlacing, Weyl inequalities, and pseudospectral intuition; and finally previews numerical algorithms including QR eigenvalue iteration, SVD numerics, least-squares stability, Krylov methods, iterative methods, and preconditioning.
