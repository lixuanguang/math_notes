\section{Spectral and Operator Structure}

Spectral and operator structure studies an operator through invariant subspaces, polynomial relations, canonical forms, structured spectral theorems, and functions of operators. The section starts with eigenvalues, eigenspaces, invariant subspaces, triangularization, and diagonalization; then develops characteristic and minimal polynomials, Cayley--Hamilton, generalized eigenspaces, nilpotent parts, and primary decomposition; then organizes Jordan and real canonical block forms; then treats normal, self-adjoint, positive, unitary, Schur, and spectral-resolution structure; and finally collects polynomial functional calculus, spectral mapping, exponentials, and square-root constructions.
