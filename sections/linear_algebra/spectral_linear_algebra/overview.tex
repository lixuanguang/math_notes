\subsection{Spectral Linear Algebra}

Spectral linear algebra studies an operator through the subspaces and polynomials that it naturally preserves. The section starts with eigenvalues, eigenvectors, eigenspaces, invariant subspaces, block criteria, commuting operators, triangularization, diagonalization, and simultaneous triangularization; then develops normal and self-adjoint spectral theory, Schur triangularization, complex and real spectral theorems, spectral resolutions, real normal block forms, positive operators, and square roots; and finally organizes the polynomial side through characteristic and minimal polynomials, Cayley--Hamilton, annihilating polynomials, generalized eigenvectors, nilpotent structure, Jordan form, and complexification-dependent material.
