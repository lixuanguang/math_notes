\section{Structural Linear Algebra}

Structural linear algebra is the coordinate-free backbone of the subject. It begins with vector spaces, subspaces, spans, independence, bases, dimension, and coordinates; then develops decompositions through sums, direct sums, complements, quotient spaces, induced maps, and dimension formulas; then studies linear maps through kernels, ranges, rank-nullity, isomorphisms, matrices of maps, change of basis, similarity, and block representation; and finally introduces dual spaces, dual maps, annihilators, transpose as the matrix of the dual map, and the multilinear viewpoint that later leads to bilinear forms and tensor products.
