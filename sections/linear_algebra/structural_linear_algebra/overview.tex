\subsection{Structural Linear Algebra}

Structural linear algebra is the coordinate-free backbone of the subject. It begins with vector spaces, subspaces, spans, independence, bases, dimension, direct sums, and quotient spaces; then develops linear maps through kernels, ranges, rank-nullity, isomorphisms, invertibility, matrices of maps, change of basis, similarity as coordinate change, and block structure; and finally introduces dual spaces, linear functionals, dual bases, annihilators, dual maps, transpose as the matrix of the dual map, and finite-dimensional biduality.
