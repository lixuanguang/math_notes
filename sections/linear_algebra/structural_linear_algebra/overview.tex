\subsection{Structural Linear Algebra}

Structural linear algebra is the coordinate-free backbone of the subject. It begins with vector spaces, subspaces, spans, independence, bases, dimension, direct sums, and quotient spaces; then develops linear maps through kernels, ranges, rank-nullity, invertibility, matrices of maps, change of basis, similarity, and block structure; and finally introduces dual spaces, linear functionals, dual maps, annihilators, and the transpose/adjoint-as-dual viewpoint.
