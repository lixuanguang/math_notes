\subsection{Geometric Linear Algebra}

Geometric linear algebra adds inner products to the structural theory. The section develops norms, orthogonality, orthogonal complements, orthonormal bases, Gram--Schmidt, Bessel and Parseval identities, Riesz representation, and Gram matrices; then uses this geometry to study projections, best approximation, least squares, normal equations, full-rank projection formulas, minimum-norm solutions, adjoints, self-adjoint projections, isometries, unitary and orthogonal maps, unitary similarity, and Householder reflections.
