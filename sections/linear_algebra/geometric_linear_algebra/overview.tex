\section{Geometric Linear Algebra}

Geometric linear algebra adds metric and angle structure to linear algebra. It first develops inner products, norms, Cauchy--Schwarz, orthogonality, orthonormal bases, Gram--Schmidt, Riesz representation, and Gram matrices; then studies orthogonal complements, projection decompositions, projection maps, and best approximation; then turns projection theory into least squares, normal equations, full-rank formulas, and minimum-norm solutions; and finally develops adjoints, conjugate transpose, self-adjoint maps, isometries, unitary and orthogonal maps, and Householder reflections.
