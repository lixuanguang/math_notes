\part{Linear and Operator Structures}

Linear algebra is organized here as a progression from structure to geometry, then to spectra, and finally to matrix analysis. The structural part builds vector spaces, linear maps, and duality without depending on coordinates. The geometric part adds inner products, orthogonality, projections, least squares, adjoints, and isometries. The spectral part studies operators through eigenstructure, invariant subspaces, spectral theorems, and polynomial/Jordan structure. Matrix analysis then returns to concrete matrices, factorizations, norms, perturbation ideas, and numerical consequences.\\
References from \cite{garcia2017second} and \cite{axler2024linear}.
