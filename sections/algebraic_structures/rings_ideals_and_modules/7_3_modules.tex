\subsection{Modules}

\begin{perspective} \label{per:dimension-as-field-case-of-rank} \hlt{Dimension as the Field Case of Rank}\\
Vector spaces over $\mathbb{F}$ are the field case of free modules. Theorem \ref{thm:basis-length-well-defined} makes finite basis length intrinsic, so dimension is well-defined; over general rings, the analogous rank theorem needs extra hypotheses.
\end{perspective}
