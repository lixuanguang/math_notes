# Quantitative-Finance Domain Overlay

When relevant, identify:

- market or asset universe, time/indexing convention, probability measure and filtration;
- model dynamics or statistical model, admissibility and no-arbitrage constraints;
- payoff, objective, loss, pricing/hedging/risk/calibration/backtest task, and inputs/outputs;
- data source, sample window, rebalancing frequency, benchmark, metric, transaction costs, liquidity, and constraints;
- calibration, purpose- and materiality-sensitive validation, outcome analysis/monitoring, robustness, uncertainty, code/data availability, intended-use boundary, and model-risk limitations;
- whether each claim is model-implied, simulated, empirical, backtested, or interpretive.

Check look-ahead, survivorship, and data-snooping controls when performance depends on them. Never turn backtest performance into a theorem, forecast, investment recommendation, or claim stronger than the source supports.
