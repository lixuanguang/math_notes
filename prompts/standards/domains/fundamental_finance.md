# Fundamental-Finance Domain Overlay

When relevant, identify:

- entity, security, industry, reporting or valuation purpose, date, currency, fiscal period, and accounting standard/basis;
- source data, variable definitions, adjustments, and historical/estimate/forecast status;
- accounting or valuation approach, key assumptions and drivers, discount/growth/multiple choices, and forecast horizon;
- scenarios, sensitivities, earnings/cash-flow quality and variability, risks, conflicts, and independence limitations;
- whether each conclusion is fact, estimate, forecast, opinion, or recommendation.

Do not present a valuation as exact, turn an empirical association or accounting convention into a theorem, or imply investment advice beyond the source and educational context.
