# Mathematics Domain Overlay

- State exact objects, ambient structures, domains, hypotheses, quantifiers, conclusions, proof architecture, and reusable constructions.
- A theorem-style claim requires a complete proof from definitions, earlier active theorem-style results, or source-supported proof material.
- Keep statement generality proportional to the source and useful local ownership. Do not add parameters, constants, compact sets, iterates, or estimates solely because the proof uses them.
- Examples, counterexamples, intuitions, remarks, figures, and methods may explain a result but are not proof authority.
- Keep empirical, physical, financial, and valuation claims out of theorem-style environments unless they are converted into explicit mathematical assumptions and the stated consequence is proved.
