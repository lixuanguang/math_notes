# Materials Science and Engineering Domain Overlay

Use this overlay for composition–processing–structure–property–performance relations, materials synthesis, characterization, property testing, degradation, manufacturing, and computational materials work.

## Material and specimen identity

- Identify material class, exact composition and fraction basis, grade/standard, supplier, batch/lot, purity, form, dimensions, orientation, location within the parent material, and condition.
- Record the complete processing and sample history that can affect the result: synthesis, forming/build route, thermal/mechanical treatment, atmosphere, cooling, surface preparation, storage, aging, irradiation, and prior tests. “As received,” “annealed,” or an alloy name alone is rarely a reproducible state.
- Distinguish nominal from measured composition, feedstock from final material, bulk from local structure, phase identity from phase fraction, and material property from component performance.

## Structure, characterization, and property tests

- For microstructure or characterization, state technique, specimen preparation, sampled location/orientation, scale and coordinate frame, instrument/model, calibration/reference, acquisition settings, spatial/spectral/temporal resolution, detection limits, and processing/segmentation choices.
- For a property test, cite the exact method/standard and revision; record specimen geometry and orientation, preparation, fixture, loading or scan rate, temperature/environment, number of specimens/repeats, failure or censoring rule, raw-to-derived calculation, uncertainty, and deviations from the method.
- Keep processing, structure, property, and performance claims linked but distinct. A micrograph, phase calculation, coupon test, or typical datasheet value does not by itself establish component-scale life, reliability, qualification, or a guaranteed design value.
- Every image or map states scale, field of view, orientation, contrast/color meaning, acquisition and processing context, and whether it is representative. Report selection, stitching, filtering, thresholding, and false coloring that could change interpretation.

## Models, data, and engineering use

- For computational work, record model class, interatomic potential/thermodynamic or kinetic database and release, software/version, composition and initial structure, cell/mesh, boundary conditions, ensemble, timestep/convergence, sampling, seeds, and comparison with experiment or benchmark.
- Curate enough metadata to reconnect composition, processing history, specimen, method, instrument, raw data, derived data, uncertainty, and persistent identifier. Preserve units, coordinate systems, ontology/template version, ownership/license, and access limits.
- For design or selection, state service environment, load/time/temperature regime, degradation mechanism, target and constraints, safety factors/allowables source, variability, and scale-up limitations. Do not infer standards compliance or fitness for service from research data alone.
- Load chemistry for chemical identity/reactions, physics for governing mechanisms, and the matching engineering/computer-science overlay for coupled hardware or computation.
