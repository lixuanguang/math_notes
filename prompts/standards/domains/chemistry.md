# Chemistry Domain Overlay

Use this overlay for chemical identity and nomenclature, reactions and mechanisms, synthesis, analytical/physical chemistry, spectroscopy, characterization, and computational chemistry.

## Identity, quantities, and reaction meaning

- Identify each material by an unambiguous name, formula or structure, stereochemical/isotopic state, charge, source, grade/purity, and identifier when useful. Distinguish substance, species, sample, solution, phase, and formulation.
- Follow current IUPAC terminology, nomenclature, quantity symbols, and units. State amount/composition basis, concentration convention, standard state, temperature, pressure, solvent, pH/ionic strength, and reference electrode or spectral reference when relevant.
- Distinguish a stoichiometric relation, net reaction, equilibrium, proposed elementary step, and mechanism. Balance atoms, charge, and labeled isotopes; place conditions/catalysts over the arrow and avoid implying that a balanced equation proves a mechanism.

## Experimental and characterization record

- A reproducible procedure records scale, reagent amounts/equivalents and concentrations, vessel/apparatus, atmosphere, order and rate of addition, mixing, temperature profile, pressure, time/end point, workup, isolation, purification, storage, and critical observations.
- Report yield with basis and product state; distinguish isolated, assay, conversion, selectivity, recovery, and theoretical yield. Record replicates/variability and unusual supplier, vessel, moisture, light, or handling effects that control the outcome.
- Characterization states technique, instrument/model, calibration/reference, acquisition and processing settings, solvent/matrix, temperature, uncertainty/resolution, and raw-data handle. Use orthogonal evidence appropriate to establish identity and purity; do not make a peak list carry a stronger conclusion than the method supports.
- For spectra/chromatograms, label axes/units, reference, peak assignment status, normalization/baseline/smoothing, integration method, and exclusions. Preserve unprocessed data when available and distinguish observed peaks from assigned structure.

## Computation, mechanism, and safety

- For computational chemistry, record software/version, method/functional, basis set or potential, pseudopotential, charge/multiplicity, geometry and constraints, solvation/relativistic/dispersion treatment, convergence, frequency/thermal corrections, and validation.
- Mark a mechanism as observed, source-proposed, computed, inferred, or speculative; state kinetic/isotopic/spectroscopic evidence, alternatives, and rate-determining or selectivity claims only to the supported degree.
- Record known hazards, incompatibilities, energetic/pressure risks, exposure controls, waste constraints, and scale dependence from authoritative sources. Never infer that a procedure is safe because the source omits a warning; notes do not replace risk assessment, training, or local rules.
- Load materials science for processing–structure–property claims and chemical engineering for process-scale balances, equipment, control, or process safety.
