# Economics Domain Overlay

Use this overlay for microeconomics, macroeconomics, econometrics, public and development economics, economic history, experiments, forecasting, policy analysis, and theoretical or computational models. Quantitative finance remains in its narrower overlay.

## Claim, model, and equilibrium

- Classify claims as definition/accounting identity, theoretical proposition, model implication, comparative static, equilibrium result, welfare statement, descriptive estimate, causal estimate, forecast/scenario, or policy judgment.
- A theoretical model states agents, goods/resources, timing, information, preferences/technology, constraints, institutions, uncertainty, market-clearing or solution concept, parameter domains, and closure conditions. Identify endogenous versus exogenous objects.
- State equilibrium existence, uniqueness, efficiency, stability, identification, or comparative statics only with the exact assumptions and proof/source support. Distinguish positive predictions from normative welfare criteria and partial- from general-equilibrium conclusions.

## Economic data and empirical design

- Record provider, series/table identifier, release and vintage, population/unit, geography, frequency, sample period, seasonal/calendar adjustment, revisions, transformations, missingness, and aggregation.
- State currency and price basis: nominal/real, deflator and reference/base year, local/constant/international currency, exchange rate or PPP method, per-capita or aggregate, annualized or period rate, stock or flow. Never compare unmatched definitions silently.
- For empirical work, state outcome, treatment/exposure, estimand, design and identification strategy, identifying assumptions, controls/fixed effects, sampling/weights, standard-error and clustering level, uncertainty, diagnostics, and sensitivity. Separate descriptive fit, structural interpretation, and causal inference.
- For experiments, state design/registration when applicable, subject selection, treatment assignment, incentives, instructions, exclusions, ethics status when stated, and analysis plan. For surveys, also apply the political-science survey disclosure standard.

## Policy, forecasts, and reproducibility

- A policy analysis identifies baseline and counterfactual, affected groups, incidence, behavioral and market responses, fiscal/resource constraint, externalities, transition path, distributional effects, and uncertainty. Separate efficiency, equity, political feasibility, and the note author's value judgment.
- A forecast or scenario states information cutoff, data vintage, horizon, conditioning assumptions, model/version, update rule, uncertainty interval, and evaluation benchmark. It is not a theorem or unconditional prediction.
- Record code, data, software/version, hardware when material, seeds, master execution order, program-to-output mapping, repository identifier, legality/access restrictions, and deviations from the source analysis. Do not call results reproduced without an independent reproduction record.
- Load math for formal results, computer science for substantive computational artifacts, history for archival economic history, and political science for institutional or survey claims.
