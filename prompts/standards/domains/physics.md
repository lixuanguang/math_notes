# Physics Domain Overlay

When relevant, identify:

- physical system, regime, coordinates and sign convention;
- quantities with units and dimensions, governing laws/equations, and dimensionless parameters;
- assumptions and idealizations, initial/boundary/interface conditions, retained and dropped terms;
- derivation route, experimental or simulation setup, uncertainty/error, data/code/material availability, and validity limits.

Keep law, model, derivation, approximation, dimensional check, simulation, experiment, and theorem roles distinct. Dimensional consistency is an error screen, not proof that a law or model is true. Do not omit scale, units, processing context, or uncertainty when they affect interpretation.
