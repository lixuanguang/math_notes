\section{Prototype and Nearest-Neighbor Methods}

Prototype and nearest-neighbor classifiers are model-free rules built from distances in feature space.  They can fit irregular decision boundaries and are often strong predictors, but their behavior is controlled by geometry: scaling, metric choice, neighborhood size, and storage of representative points.  The local-averaging view appears in Method~\ref{meth:sl-nearest-neighbor-local-averaging}; this section records the classification and metric-design side.
